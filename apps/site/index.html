Hear about good things nearby. Without being tracked.

Proxim lets local businesses show a short message inside apps you already use, when you are close to them. Your phone works out where you are by itself. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What it looks like

Your bus app

Next bus: 12 min

You are walking to the bus stop past a café. A small, clearly labelled note appears in the app you already have open. No pop-up, no alert on your lock screen, and no ad that follows you around the internet afterwards.

How it works

  1. A local business writes a short message and marks its spot on a map.
  2. A real person checks it before it goes live.
  3. Your phone shows it when you are nearby. Your phone decides on its own, so where you go stays on your phone.

Who it is for

Local businesses

Reach people who are walking or driving near you right now, with small budgets and simple prices.

App makers

Earn money from local ads that respect your users. You see exactly what you earn and what we keep.

Everyone

Find out about useful things around you. We keep no profile of you and have nothing about you to sell.

Fair for small businesses

Online ads are usually stacked in favour of big companies, and a large share of the money goes to middlemen. We do it differently.

  • Same price for everyone. The corner shop pays the same rate as the big chain.
  • Paying more does not put you on top. A bigger budget runs for longer. It does not push other businesses aside.
  • No middlemen. Businesses deal with us directly, so more of the money reaches the app.
  • Same checks for everyone. Every message goes through the same review.
  • We never sell information about people. Businesses only see totals, such as how many people saw their message.
