For local businesses

Reach the right people, right outside your door, at the right time.

Proxim puts your message in front of people who are near your business or heading your way, inside apps they already use. Show it at lunchtime, on the morning commute, or only on match days. You pay only when people near you see it.

Why it is good value

  • Only people nearby. Your message reaches people close enough to walk in, not the whole city.
  • Only when it matters. Choose the days and hours your offer applies, and it shows only then.
  • Only pay for real views. You are charged when people near you see your message, not for empty airtime.
  • Start small. Prepay a small budget and stop whenever you like. No contracts, no agency.
  • See what you got. A simple weekly report shows how many people saw your message.

How it works

  1. Write a short message and mark your spot on a map.
  2. Choose when it runs and how much you want to spend.
  3. We check it and switch it on. A real person reviews every message before it goes live.

Fair for small businesses

  • Same price for everyone. The corner shop pays the same rate as the big chain.
  • Paying more does not put you on top. A bigger budget runs for longer. It does not push other businesses aside.
  • No middlemen. You deal with us directly, so less of your money is lost along the way.

Where we are

Proxim is still being built and nothing is for sale yet. We plan to start with a small, invitation-only trial in one city.
