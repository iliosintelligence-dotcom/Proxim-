How it works

Up and running in three steps.

  1. Write your message

    Keep it short, then draw your area on a map: the road outside, a bridge, or your front door.

  2. Choose when it runs

    Pick the days, the hours and how much you want to spend.

  3. We check it and switch it on

    A real person reviews every message, then a weekly report shows how many people saw it.
