Proxim In development · pilot by invitation

Signposts, not surveillance.

Proxim puts short, labelled local messages inside the apps people already use, at the moment they are near a place or heading toward it. Like a roadside sign, it knows where it is, not who is looking.

How it works

  1. A local business draws a place. A shop, a café or a council marks a spot, an area or a stretch of road and writes a short message.
  2. Every message is reviewed and signed. A person approves each one before it runs. The phone checks the signature before it shows anything.
  3. The phone decides, on the phone. The partner app's copy of Proxim matches its own location against signed local packs. Raw location never leaves the device.

The billboard principle

A signpost may depend on where the phone is, where it is heading, the time of day and the kind of app it appears in. It never depends on who the person is. Proxim has no advertising IDs, no profiles and no user data to sell.

Fair access for every advertiser

Up to 40% of a typical online ad budget goes to middlemen. Proxim takes a direct path and holds itself to seven rules.

  • Direct path. Advertiser to Proxim to the app. No exchanges, no resellers.
  • One public rate card. The same price for every advertiser, large or small.
  • Money never buys rank. Relevance decides what shows. A bigger budget runs longer, not higher.
  • Published fee split. Every statement shows what the app earns and what Proxim keeps.
  • Low entry. Prepaid, with a small minimum, so a corner shop can take part.
  • Same review for everyone. Every advertiser gets the same checks and the same response time.
  • No user data for sale. Advertisers see only grouped counts, never people.

Where we are

The on-device decision engine and its simulators are built and tested. Signed delivery is in progress. The advertiser console, app SDKs and billing come next, followed by an invitation-only pilot in one city. Nothing is on sale yet.
