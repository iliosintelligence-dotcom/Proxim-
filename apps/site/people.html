For people who use apps

Hear about good things nearby. Without being tracked.

Some apps you use may show a short note from a local business when you are close by. Your phone works out where you are by itself. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else.

A small, clearly labelled note inside an app you already have open. No pop-up, no alert on your lock screen, and no ad that follows you around the internet afterwards.

Our promises to you

  • Your location stays on your phone. Your phone decides what to show. We never receive where you are or where you have been.
  • No profile of you. What you see depends on where you are and the time of day, never on who you are.
  • Nothing about you to sell. Businesses only see totals, such as how many people saw their message.
  • Always labelled. Every note is marked as an ad, and a real person checks each one before it runs.
