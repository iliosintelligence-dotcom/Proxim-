For app makers

Earn money from your app with local ads your users won't mind.

Add Proxim to your app and show short, useful messages from businesses near your users. You keep most of the money, and your users get ads that respect them.

We are starting with phone apps and websites. Smart TV and desktop apps come next.

You keep most of the money

65–70%

We plan to pay app makers 65 to 70% of what businesses spend, and every statement shows the exact split. With most other ad networks, a large part of the money goes to middlemen before it reaches you.

Ads your users won't mind

  • Not repetitive. The same ad is not shown over and over. Each person sees it a limited number of times.
  • Not invasive. Short, clearly labelled text that sits inside your app. No pop-ups, no autoplay video, no sound.
  • Relevant to your app. You choose which kinds of business and offers are allowed, so ads fit your app and your content, and are useful to the people who use it.
  • Checked by a person. Every ad is reviewed before it can appear in your app.
  • Private by design. Your users' location stays on their device. We never receive it, and nothing about your users is shared or sold.

How it works

  1. Add our toolkit to your app.
  2. Choose which kinds of ads you allow and where they appear.
  3. Keep most of the money. A simple statement shows what you earned and what we kept.
