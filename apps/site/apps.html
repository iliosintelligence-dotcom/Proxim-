What you get

  • A fair, published share. Every statement shows what you earned and what Proxim kept. No hidden fees and no middlemen.
  • Ads that fit your app. Short, clearly labelled text messages. No pop-ups and no autoplay video.
  • You stay in control. Choose where messages appear and which kinds of business you accept.
  • Every ad checked by a person before it can appear in your app.
  • Your users' location stays on their device. We never receive it, and we build no profiles, so nothing about your users is shared or sold.

How it works

  1. Add our toolkit to your app.
  2. Choose where messages appear and which kinds of business you accept.
  3. Earn from every view. A simple statement shows what you made.
