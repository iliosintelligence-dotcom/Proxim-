Why your users won't mind

Ads that feel like help, not noise.

  • Repetition

    Not repetitive

    The same ad is not shown over and over. Each person sees it a limited number of times.

  • Comfort

    Not invasive

    Short, clearly labelled text inside your app. No pop-ups, no autoplay video, no sound.

  • Relevance

    Relevant to your app

    You choose which kinds of business and offers are allowed, so every ad fits your app, your content and your users.

    Private by design

    Your users’ location stays on their device. We never receive it, and nothing about your users is shared or sold.
